Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from students and parents on Shelfly.

What is Shelfly?

Shelfly is a student book-sharing and donation platform built to help students give educational books a second life and learn without financial barriers.

Is Shelfly free?

Yes, Shelfly is 100% free. Every book listed here is donated or exchanged voluntarily. Asking for money after listing a free book violates our Community Rules.

Can I sell books here?

No. Shelfly only supports free donations and book exchanges. If you wish to sell books, Shelfly is not the correct platform.

How do book exchanges work?

Donors list books they want to share, and students can search for them on the platform. When a match is found, you contact the donor directly using their provided contact method to arrange a safe, public hand-off.
